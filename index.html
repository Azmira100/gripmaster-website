Our Story

Built for Every Hand.
Every Kitchen.

OpenEase is a universal jar and bottle opener designed to help users open containers more easily with one hand. Its dual V-shaped rubber grip system adjusts to different container sizes, creating a secure, non-slip hold for jars and bottles.

We designed OpenEase because everyone deserves to cook independently — whether you have arthritis, limited grip strength, or just encounter a particularly stubborn lid. No tools, no second hand, no struggle.

  • Fits containers from 1.5" to 4.5" wide
  • Self-adjusting V-grip — no settings needed
  • Designed for seniors, arthritis sufferers & everyone
  • Dual slot design for two containers at once
  • Food-safe, BPA-free materials
