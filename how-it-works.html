Step-by-Step

How GripMaster Works

Four simple steps. No tools. No adjustments. Just place your jar, let the grips do their thing, and twist.

The Process

Step by Step

1

Place Your Jar or Bottle

Slide your jar or bottle down into one of GripMaster's two V-shaped slots. The opening is wide at the top, so you don't need to aim precisely — just lower it in and let gravity do the guiding.

💡 Works with jars from 1.5" to 4.5" wide — pasta sauces, pickle jars, salsa, jam, and more.
2

Let the Grip Lock In

As the container slides down into the tapered channel, the rubber fins automatically compress against the sides. The V-shape creates even pressure from both sides simultaneously — no manual adjustment needed.

💡 The heavier the jar, the stronger the grip — GripMaster actually grips tighter under load.
3

Twist the Lid

With the jar locked in place by the grips, use your hand to grip the lid and turn counterclockwise. Because the jar can't rotate, all of your turning force goes directly into breaking the vacuum seal — not wasted fighting the jar itself.

💡 For vacuum-sealed jars, you may hear a satisfying "pop" as the seal breaks — that's normal and good!
4

Open Effortlessly

Lift off the lid — done! Remove your jar from the GripMaster slot and enjoy your food. The rubber fins retract when the jar is lifted, ready for the next container.

💡 The dual-slot design means you can have two jars ready at once — great for cooking multiple dishes.
The Engineering

The Science Behind the Grip

GripMaster isn't magic — it's physics. The V-shaped tapered channel converts the downward weight of the jar into lateral clamping pressure on the container walls. Here's how it all works together:

📐

V-Taper GeometryAs the jar slides deeper, the narrowing channel forces rubber fins inward from both sides, self-centering the container and distributing grip evenly.

🔁

Counter-Torque MechanismWhen you twist the lid clockwise (to tighten) or counterclockwise (to open), the grip actually increases — torque pushes the jar deeper into the channel.

🧱

Non-Slip BaseA rubber-rimmed base prevents the entire unit from sliding on the countertop while you apply opening force.

Pro Tips

Get the Most Out of Your GripMaster

Warm the Lid First

For extremely stubborn vacuum-sealed jars, run warm water over the lid for 30 seconds before using GripMaster. This expands the metal slightly and makes opening easier.

Center the Container

Let the jar slide all the way down until it stops moving. A centered jar means the grip is maximized on both sides evenly.

Clean Regularly

Wipe down the rubber fins every few weeks to maintain maximum grip. Food residue on the fins can reduce friction over time.

Use Both Slots

The dual-slot design lets you have two containers ready at once — perfect for prepping multiple sauces or condiments during cooking.

Don't Force It

If a lid doesn't budge, don't apply extreme force. Try the warm water trick first, or use the claw attachment (on select models) for extra torque.
