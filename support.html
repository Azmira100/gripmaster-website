Warranty & Returns

🛡️

1-Year Limited Warranty

GripMaster products are covered against manufacturing defects for one year from the date of purchase.

  • Defective rubber fins that crack or tear under normal use
  • Shell cracking due to material failure (not impact damage)
  • Grip mechanism failure that prevents normal operation
  • Any other manufacturing defect

To claim warranty, contact us with your order number and a photo of the issue. We'll send a replacement at no charge.

🔄

30-Day Returns

Not satisfied? Return your GripMaster within 30 days of delivery for a full refund — no questions asked.

  • Item must be in original or near-original condition
  • Include your order number with the return
  • Amazon orders are returned through Amazon's standard process
  • Refunds processed within 3–5 business days of receipt

For Amazon purchases, initiate your return through your Amazon account under "My Orders." For direct purchases, contact us at support@gripmaster.com.
